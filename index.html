Privacy Policy for Voxlist AI

This Privacy Policy describes how Voxlist AI (voxlistai) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

Effective Date: November 20, 2026 Developer: Muhammad Usman Kashif Package Name: voxlistai

1. Overview and Scope

Muhammad Usman Kashif built the Voxlist AI mobile application (package identifier: voxlistai) as a productivity and task-management tool. Voxlist AI allows users to speak naturally in any language or enter text notes to generate structured English tasks with due dates, times, subtasks, and reminders.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use Voxlist AI, how that information is used, the permissions requested on your Android device, and your choices regarding your data. By creating an account or using Voxlist AI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to operate and maintain the core features of Voxlist AI:

A. Account and Authentication Information

  • Sign-Up and Login Details: When you create an account or sign in to Voxlist AI, we collect your email address and encrypted authentication credentials to authenticate your account and sync your tasks.

B. Voice Recordings (Audio Data)

  • User-Initiated Voice Notes: When you press or hold the microphone button to speak a task in your preferred language, Voxlist AI records your audio clip and transmits it securely over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) for speech-to-text transcription and AI task generation into English.
  • No Background Recording: Voxlist AI never accesses your microphone in the background. Audio is recorded only when you actively trigger voice input.

C. Tasks, Subtasks, and User Notes

  • Task Content: Typed notes, AI-generated task titles, subtasks, scheduled dates and times, reminder settings, completion status, and task categories (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Upcoming, and No Date).
  • Storage and Sync: Tasks are stored on your device and synced with our database so your task list stays accessible across sessions.

D. AI Mistake Reports and Feedback

  • Optional Issue Reporting: If you use the Report AI Mistake option inside the Settings screen, we collect the input note, generated task output, and any feedback description you submit to help improve task generation accuracy.

E. Device and App Diagnostics

  • Technical Logs: Basic diagnostic information such as device model, Android OS version, app version, and crash logs to maintain stability and deliver scheduled task notifications reliably.
No Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, voice recordings, or task lists to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

3. Android App Permissions

Voxlist AI requests only the device permissions needed for its features to work:

Permission Purpose in Voxlist AI When It Is Used
RECORD_AUDIO
(Microphone)		 Allows you to speak voice notes in any language to generate English tasks. Only while you actively press or hold the voice input button.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS & Alarms Sends reminders and notifications for tasks with scheduled dates and times. When a task reminder you created or confirmed reaches its scheduled time.
INTERNET & Network State Connects securely to speech-to-text AI services, authentication servers, and task database sync. During login, voice/text AI task creation, syncing, and submitting AI mistake reports.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • Task Generation and Translation: Converting multilingual voice notes and text inputs into structured English tasks and subtasks.
  • Reminders and Organization: Grouping tasks by schedule (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, Upcoming, No Date) and triggering reminders on time.
  • Account Management: Authenticating your login sessions and keeping your tasks synced with your account.
  • Quality and Reliability: Reviewing AI mistakes reported through Settings and fixing technical bugs or crashes.

5. Third-Party Services and AI Processing

To provide voice transcription, natural-language task extraction, and secure authentication and database storage, Voxlist AI uses trusted third-party cloud infrastructure and AI API providers.

  • Encrypted Transmission: All audio clips, text notes, and account requests are transmitted using standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Restricted Processing: Third-party providers process data strictly on our behalf to perform the requested task-creation or authentication function and are not permitted to use your data for advertising or profiling.
  • No Third-Party Ad SDKs: Voxlist AI does not include third-party behavioral advertising trackers.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your account profile and saved tasks only while your account remains active. Voice audio clips sent for transcription are processed temporarily to generate text and are not permanently stored as public audio files.

In-App Account and Data Deletion:
  • Delete Individual Tasks: You can edit or delete any individual task or subtask directly inside the app at any time.
  • Delete Account: You can permanently delete your account yourself directly inside the Voxlist AI app by opening Settings and selecting the Delete Account option. Confirming this action immediately and permanently removes your account and all associated tasks, subtasks, and user data from our database.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and administrative safeguards—including encrypted network transport (HTTPS/TLS), secure token-based authentication, and restricted database access rules—to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

8. Children's Privacy

Voxlist AI is a general-audience productivity application and is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through Voxlist AI, please contact us so we can remove the information.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your region (including GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and applicable local laws), you have the right to:

  • Access and review the tasks and account details stored in your Voxlist AI account.
  • Edit or delete your tasks and subtasks directly inside the app.
  • Revoke microphone or notification permissions at any time through your Android device's Settings > Apps > Voxlist AI > Permissions.
  • Permanently delete your account and database records directly from the app's Settings screen.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as we improve Voxlist AI or adapt to legal and platform requirements. When updates are made, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this page. Continued use of Voxlist AI after updates indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
